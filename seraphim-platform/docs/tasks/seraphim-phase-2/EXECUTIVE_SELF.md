# Seraphim Executive Self

Seraphim is the executive identity and centralized judgment layer. A decision evaluates mission relevance, evidence quality, source reliability, historical calibration, novelty, consistency, contradictions, risk, urgency, operator policy, memory context, and prediction performance.

## Executive Intuition

Executive intuition is bounded pattern recognition under incomplete, conflicting, stale, or strategically inadequate evidence. It is neither randomness nor a policy bypass.

| Required record | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Context and evidence | What was available at decision time. |
| Advice and rejection | Which advisory signals were considered or rejected. |
| Uncertainty and expected outcome | Why the decision was not presented as certainty. |
| Observed outcome and calibration | What later happened and how weighting changed. |

Intuition cannot bypass an operator prohibition, required approval, audit control, privacy boundary, security control, or claim protection.
