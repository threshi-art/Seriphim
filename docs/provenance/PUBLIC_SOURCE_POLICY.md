# Public Source and Provenance Policy

## Principle

Possessing a document does not grant permission to republish it. This
repository preserves reusable doctrine through original summaries, citations,
and source metadata—not by copying an entire private research library.

## Source Register Fields

Any future public source register should include:

- title and authoring organization;
- publication date and stable public location;
- document version or identifier;
- rights or distribution status;
- date accessed;
- principles derived for Seraphim/EiRAM;
- transformations performed;
- restrictions and review notes.

## Generally Acceptable

- Original project documentation and code with clear ownership.
- Public-domain government material after classification and distribution
  markings are checked.
- Short quotations and citations used within applicable legal limits.
- Original summaries of analytic methods with explicit attribution.
- Synthetic data and examples.

## Metadata or Citation Only

- Public documents with unclear redistribution rights.
- Large reference works not required to build or test the software.
- Historical manuals useful only for critical, ethical, or defensive context.
- Standards available under controlled or paid distribution.

## Excluded

- Clinical manuals and assessment instruments.
- Personal psychological profiles and case dossiers.
- Restricted, export-controlled, classified, proprietary, or leaked material.
- Duplicate PDFs and format conversions.
- Raw chat exports and private Agent knowledge files.
- Third-party images, audio, or video without documented rights.
- Coercive interrogation or exploitation material presented as operational
  instruction.

Every imported asset requires an identifiable owner, reason for inclusion,
rights basis, and removal path.
