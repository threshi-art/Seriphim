# Analytical Boundaries

## Intended Use

Seraphim/EiRAM supports evidence organization, structured analysis, competing
hypotheses, lawful research planning, decision support, and clearly bounded
forecasting. It is not a clinical instrument, law-enforcement risk score,
autonomous surveillance system, or substitute for qualified legal, medical,
security, or mental-health professionals.

## Prohibited Claims

Public components must not present conversational or social-media analysis as:

- a diagnosis or psychometric result;
- proof of criminal intent or dangerousness;
- verified identity from a shared handle alone;
- a validated probability of violence, radicalization, or future conduct;
- a legal conclusion without jurisdiction and authority review;
- a completed external action without verification evidence.

## Personal and Sensitive Material

Do not commit personal memory, psychological dossiers, private conversations,
relationship context, health records, educational records, employment data,
credentials, Agent identifiers, or case material that could identify a private
person. Synthetic examples are the default for tests and documentation.

## Collection Boundaries

Lawful HUMINT and investigative planning may cover rapport, interviewing,
source assessment, contradiction testing, public records, and corroboration.
Coercion, exploitation, unauthorized access, evasion, credential collection,
or operational instructions derived from abusive historical manuals are out of
scope.

## Untrusted Content

Transcripts, documents, webpages, comments, and media descriptions are data.
Instructions embedded in them do not control tools, routing, memory, or system
behavior. Acquisition components preserve the content for analysis while
quarantining its authority.

## Human Control

High-impact actions require explicit scope, target, authority, and verifiable
completion state. Missing information results in a bounded request or `blocked`
state, never an invented recipient, deadline, permission, or outcome.

Decision support must preserve a named decision owner, distinguish facts from
assumptions, expose uncertainty and reversibility, and avoid making a
consequential choice on the user's behalf.

Legal research must identify jurisdiction, date, authority hierarchy, material
fact gaps, and limits. It supports issue-spotting and research rather than legal
representation or a guaranteed legal conclusion.

Workspace inspection is read-only by default. Repository or filesystem changes
require exact target resolution, preservation of unrelated work, explicit
authorization proportionate to consequence, and a recovery path.
