# Decision Record Contract

Use this structure unless the decision owner requests a compatible format. Keep facts, assumptions, and uncertainties in separate fields.

## 1. Decision frame

- **Decision owner:** person accountable for the final choice.
- **Decision statement:** the choice to be made, in one testable sentence.
- **Time horizon:** decision deadline and the period over which consequences matter.
- **Constraints and objectives:** non-negotiables, priorities, and affected parties.

## 2. Evidence state

| Type | Record | Source or owner | Date | Confidence / uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fact | Verified observation or data | Source | Date observed | Confidence and limitation |
| Assumption | Belief used in the comparison | Owner | Date set | Rationale and test |
| Uncertainty | Unknown that could affect the result | Owner | Date logged | Low / medium / high; likelihood and impact |

Use **low**, **medium**, or **high** uncertainty explicitly. Score the likelihood and impact in plain language; do not imply measurements that do not exist.

## 3. Option comparison

List all actionable options and a genuine **status quo** option. Evaluate each against identical criteria.

| Option | Benefits and costs by criterion | Key evidence | Reversibility | Sensitivities | Disconfirming evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Status quo | Consequences of maintaining or deferring the current course | Evidence | Reversible / partially reversible / difficult to reverse; reversal cost and trigger | What could change its standing | Evidence that weakens it |
| Option A | Consequences by criterion | Evidence | Classification; reversal cost and trigger | What could change its standing | Evidence that weakens it |
| Option B | Consequences by criterion | Evidence | Classification; reversal cost and trigger | What could change its standing | Evidence that weakens it |

## 4. Support and ownership

- **Conditional recommendation or decision support:** explain which option best fits the current record, not an instruction to choose it.
- **Decision-changing conditions:** facts, assumptions, or criterion weights that would reverse or materially weaken the support.
- **Owner-retained choice:** state that the decision owner makes the final consequential choice.
- **Checkpoints:** next evidence action, accountable owner, review date or trigger, and conditions that reopen the decision.
