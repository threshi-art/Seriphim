# Quality Gates

Before delivery, verify:

1. The key question is answered.
2. The main judgment appears early.
3. Every material claim has support or is labeled as inference.
4. Assumptions are visible.
5. At least one plausible alternative was considered when uncertainty matters.
6. Confidence matches evidence quality.
7. Facts, forecasts, opinions, and recommendations are distinct.
8. Terms are consistent and abbreviations are defined.
9. The output contains no fabricated citation, quotation, date, docket entry, or test result.
10. Sensitive or proprietary information is removed or generalized.
11. Psychological material does not become an unauthorized diagnosis.
12. Protected source text is summarized rather than reproduced.
13. Headings communicate substance.
14. Repetition and generic AI phrases are removed.
15. The conclusion adds decision value.
16. The next action is concrete.

Block publication when metadata is invalid, a source is missing for a load bearing claim, an internal link is broken, or the evidence status is unclear.
