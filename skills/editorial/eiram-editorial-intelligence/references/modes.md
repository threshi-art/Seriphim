# Operating Modes

## Engineering precision

Use for procedures, requirements, verification, architecture, test plans, runbooks, and technical explanations. Define inputs, outputs, interfaces, assumptions, failure conditions, verification method, and acceptance criteria.

## Intelligence assessment

Use for uncertain, adversarial, strategic, geopolitical, organizational, or behavioral questions. Produce key judgments, confidence, sourcing, alternatives, indicators, implications, and collection gaps.

## Legal analysis

Use IRAC or a decision centered variant. Separate allegations from established facts, law from policy, holdings from dicta, and possible exposure from probable outcome. Never invent authority.

## Policy memorandum

Define the objective, current state, options, tradeoffs, implementation path, authorities, risks, metrics, and recommendation.

## Project case study

Describe problem, context, constraints, role, stakeholders, architecture, decisions, alternatives, implementation, testing, results, limitations, lessons, portfolio summary, resume bullet, and interview explanation. Sanitize proprietary information.

## Research experiment

State question, hypothesis, baseline, variables, controls, environment, procedure, evaluation criteria, results, confounders, limitations, replication method, and follow up tests.

## Video and companion content

Create thesis, audience, hook, research questions, outline, script, visual plan, demonstration, chapters, description, thumbnail concept, companion article, resource kit, and promotion copy.

## Anti slop revision

Find the real claim. Delete sentences that do not advance it. Replace abstractions with concrete facts. Preserve necessary technical vocabulary. Vary rhythm naturally. Strengthen transitions through logic. Keep legitimate personality and emotional force.

## Seraphim analytical voice

Use for strategic, philosophical, reflective, or high context writing. Permit controlled lyricism, sharp humor, and intellectual force. Require evidence, explicit assumptions, counterarguments, and practical meaning. Never let ornament replace analysis.
