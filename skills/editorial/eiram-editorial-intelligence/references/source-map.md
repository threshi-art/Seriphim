# Source Map

Use these source families when they are available in the user's connected files or workspace.

## Intelligence training and tradecraft

- DoD Intelligence and Security Training Standards: use for repeatable training design, validation, assessment, and evaluation architecture.
- A Tradecraft Primer: use for structured analytic techniques and uncertainty management.
- CIA Analytic Thinking and Presentation handbook: use for key intelligence questions, conceptualization, core assertions, inverted pyramid structure, objectivity, review, and briefing.
- Compendium of Analytic Tradecraft Notes: use for assumptions, sourcing, credibility, outlook, summaries, conclusions, and consumer relevance.
- DIA Style Manual and Analyst's Style Manual: use for concise, precise, compelling intelligence writing, grammar, abbreviations, numbers, headings, and editorial consistency.
- UK ISTAR doctrine and Intelligence Corps material: use for the distinction between collection, situational awareness, information, intelligence, and decision support. Do not reproduce restricted text.

## Engineering and systems

- DO-178C compliance and verification references: use for planning, requirements traceability, verification, independence, coverage, configuration management, quality assurance, and certification evidence.
- Predictive digital twin thesis: use for asset specific models, probabilistic updating, reduced order models, sensor assimilation, and lifecycle reasoning.
- Physics aware foundation model material: use for hybrid first principles and machine learning research framing.
- NSA network infrastructure security guide: use for defensive architecture, segmentation, access control, integrity, maintenance, and secure operations.
- Patents and speculative aerospace material: treat patent grant as proof of patent issuance, not proof that the claimed physics works. Separate legal status, engineering plausibility, and experimental evidence.

## Personal and voice context

- Loki and Seraphim reports, thread analyses, and competence essays: use as user authored or user approved voice and preference context, not as independent clinical evidence.
- Personal psychological profiles are narrative artifacts unless prepared by a qualified clinician using valid methods.

## Clinical and assessment materials

- DSM, MMPI, Beck Depression Inventory, GAD-7, and Child Behavior Checklist: use only for general education, source based explanation, or clinician directed work. Do not administer restricted instruments, reproduce protected content, diagnose, or present scores as clinical conclusions without appropriate professional involvement.

## Excluded operationalization

Do not use interrogation manuals, deception manuals, covert surveillance material, or similar sources to facilitate coercion, abuse, evasion, or wrongdoing. Limit use to historical analysis, ethics, defensive awareness, and high level critique.
