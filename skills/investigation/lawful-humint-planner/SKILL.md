# Lawful HUMINT Collection Planner

## Overview

Turn a bounded information need into a lawful, consent-aware human-source
collection plan. Protect people first: collection value never overrides
authority, consent, safety, privacy, or a source's right to stop.

## Establish the Boundary

Before planning contact, record:

- the collection objective and decision it supports;
- the lawful authority or ordinary access basis;
- who may be contacted and who is excluded;
- whether participation is informed and voluntary;
- privacy, retention, attribution, and disclosure limits;
- the facts that would require stopping or escalating.

If authority, consent, or safe access is unclear, return a bounded clarification
or `blocked`. Do not invent permission.

## Build the Plan

1. Convert the objective into answerable information gaps.
2. Identify source classes by access and knowledge, not vulnerability.
3. Prefer transparent outreach and ordinary interviewing.
4. Draft neutral questions that do not disclose sensitive third-party facts.
5. Separate source statements from collector interpretation.
6. Plan corroboration with independent, lawfully available evidence.
7. Record contradiction tests, source limitations, and confidence changes.
8. Define retention, minimization, and deletion rules before collection.

Use `references/collection-plan-contract.md` for the deliverable schema.

## Hard Limits

Do not plan or facilitate:

- pretexting, impersonation, baiting, or deceptive relationship-building;
- coercion, intimidation, blackmail, manipulation, or exploitation;
- credential, secret, medical, financial, or intimate-data harvesting;
- unauthorized access, surveillance evasion, trespass, or bypassing controls;
- targeting based on a person's distress, dependency, or protected status;
- operational guidance copied from abusive or restricted historical manuals.

Public records and consensual interviews may support the plan, but their
availability does not erase privacy or harm considerations.

## Output

Return the objective, lawful basis, consent status, source classes, proposed
questions, corroboration plan, privacy controls, risks, stop conditions,
unresolved approvals, and next safe step. Label unknowns and do not claim that
collection occurred unless verified evidence shows it did.

## Quick Reference

| Condition | Response |
|---|---|
| Clear lawful basis and voluntary contact | Produce a bounded plan |
| Consent or authority uncertain | Clarify or block |
| Deception, coercion, or unauthorized access requested | Refuse that method |
| Sensitive information is unnecessary | Minimize or omit it |
| Collection would create an external effect | Route through action control |

## Common Mistakes

- Treating rapport as permission to manipulate.
- Confusing source access with source reliability.
- Collecting broadly before defining the decision need.
- Repeating an allegation without independent corroboration.
- Publishing identifying details that the objective does not require.
