# Evidence Ladder

Use the highest available rung and explain when lower rungs are all that exist.

## Tier 1: Primary adjudicative or official records

Court filings and orders, warrants and affidavits, charging documents, booking records, official police reports, government datasets, regulator records, corporate filings, official transcripts, authenticated public records.

## Tier 2: Primary contemporaneous traces

Dispatch and CAD logs, body camera indexes, EMS calls, official incident maps, property records, timestamped business records, archived official pages, direct statements by involved parties.

## Tier 3: High quality independent reporting

Local newspapers, major news organizations, specialist legal or industry reporting that names sources or links records.

## Tier 4: First party narrative material

YouTube videos, livestreams, social posts, interviews, company statements, attorney commentary. Useful for leads and chronology but potentially interested or incomplete.

## Tier 5: Community reconstruction

Reddit, forums, reposts, comments, fan wikis, crowdsourced timelines. Use to discover names, dates, case numbers, mirrors, or source links. Do not treat as dispositive unless independently corroborated.

## Absence checks

Before saying a record is absent, verify:

1. Correct jurisdiction.
2. Correct date range.
3. Dataset coverage date.
4. Name variants or aliases.
5. Whether the offense can proceed without booking.
6. Whether records may be sealed, delayed, offline, or available only by request.
