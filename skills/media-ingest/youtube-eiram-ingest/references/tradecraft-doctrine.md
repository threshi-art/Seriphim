# Tradecraft Doctrine for Native Video Analysis

Use these principles when evidence is incomplete, contradictory, deceptive, or operationally constrained.

## Source and judgment

Be precise about what is known and how it is known. Distinguish reported information, direct observation, and analytical judgment. Consider deception without assuming deception merely because a source is partisan, edited, or wrong.

## Uncertainty

Incomplete access is normal. Give the strongest answer supported by the available record, identify assumptions and collection gaps, and recast conclusions when new evidence changes the premise. Bold conclusions require ample, scrubbed information. Ambiguous or deception prone evidence requires explicit doubt.

## Alternatives

Test key assumptions and at least one plausible alternative explanation. Seek evidence that could disconfirm the preferred interpretation. A large consistent body of copied claims can be weaker than a smaller body of genuinely independent evidence.

## Source quality

Evaluate relevance, reliability, evidentiary weight, freshness, access, and independence separately. Primary sources can still be incomplete, stale, self interested, or deceptive. Absence carries weight only when the relevant record should exist and the dataset or publication channel covers the event.

## Presentation

Lead with what the user most needs to know, then explain the evidence, uncertainty, and implications. Preserve traceability from source to claim to inference to forecast. State confidence by material judgment and explain its basis.

## Corpus basis

This doctrine distills the uploaded `A Tradecraft Primer: Structured Analytic Techniques for Improving Intelligence Analysis`, `CIA Analytic Thinking and Presentation for Intelligence Producers`, `A Compendium of Analytic Tradecraft Notes`, and `The Analyst's Style Manual`. The governing ideas are source transparency, explicit assumptions, alternative hypotheses, calibrated confidence, and separation of reported information from analyst judgment.
